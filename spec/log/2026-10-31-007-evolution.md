## Current project summary

Susi Ghiso Japan is a small private React guide for friends on their honeymoon in Japan, hosted on Vercel and hidden from crawlers. The homepage is now implemented against a real itinerary (Tokyo 5–10 Nov, Osaka 11–16, Okinawa 17–26, Tokyo 27–29, back in Italy 30 Nov; Osaka length and the Okinawa → Tokyo date to be confirmed). The layout has moved on from the approved spec: clock and yen widget share a row, the checklists are one section with a Preflight | Flight switch, preflight disappears from landing day, and the yen widget leads with a large 1 € = N ¥ rate and keeps only the JPY → EUR table. The user has extended the privacy rule: browsers may call free data sources (Frankfurter for the yen rate, Open-Meteo for a Fuji visibility tool) when only generic request data such as the IP address is exposed. These changes contradict R5, R6 and the build-time-only data approach; they await a refreshed proposal and approval before current-state.md and the blueprints are updated.
