## IC card first

Buy a **Suica** or **Pasmo** (or add one to your phone wallet) as soon as you land. It works on trains, metro, buses and most konbini. Top it up at any station machine.

## Offline maps

Before you lose signal on the move, download the city area in Google Maps. Once saved it costs no data, and it still gives walking and driving directions. For trains, check the station board or a transit app that works offline.

> Tip: switch off background app refresh while on e-SIM. It quietly uses data.

## Trains

Most journeys are easier by train than by taxi. Check the platform on the board, not only the timetable on your phone: platforms change.
