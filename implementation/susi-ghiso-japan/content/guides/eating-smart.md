## Tipping

Tipping is not expected. Just pay at the register or the machine. Leaving extra cash can confuse staff.

## Ordering at small places

Many small restaurants use a **ticket machine** at the entrance. Pick your dishes, pay, and hand the ticket to the staff. If the machine is in Japanese, look for the photos.

## Finding good places

Look for places full of locals at lunchtime and small queues, rather than the ones with photo menus on the street.
