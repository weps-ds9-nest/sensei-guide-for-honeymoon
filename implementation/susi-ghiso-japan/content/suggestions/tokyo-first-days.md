Example route: a slow first day in the old and the new city. Each `##` heading becomes a numbered stop.

## Asakusa at sunrise

Walk to the temple before the crowds. Stop for a snack on the way out.

## Shimokitazawa for lunch

Narrow streets, vintage shops and small cafés. Go hungry and order from a counter.

## Shibuya at dusk

Cross the scramble, then find the quieter streets behind the station for the lights.
