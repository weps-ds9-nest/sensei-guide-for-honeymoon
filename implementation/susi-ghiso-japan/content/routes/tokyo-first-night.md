You land tired, but the first night in Tokyo is worth staying up for. Four easy stages, all within a few train stops of Shinjuku.

## Landing and getting to the hotel

![Narita Express at Shibuya station](/media/routes/narita-express.jpg "Photo: Syced, CC0, Wikimedia Commons")

Get your IC card (Suica or Pasmo) right after immigration. From Narita take the **Narita Express** straight to Shinjuku; from Haneda the Keikyu line and a change at Shinagawa is quickest. Drop the bags, shower, go out.

## Shinjuku and the first shopping

![Neon street signs in Shinjuku at blue hour](/media/routes/shinjuku-neon.jpg "Photo: Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons")

Walk around the station and stock up on the basics:

- **Konbini** (7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson): water, onigiri, toiletries, a cheap umbrella.
- **100 yen shop** (Daiso, Seria): adapters, a small towel, a spare bag.
- **Goshuincho**: the stamp book for temples and shrines. Buy one now, the first shrine visit will thank you.

## Dinner at Omoide Yokocho

![Lanterns in Omoide Yokocho](/media/routes/omoide-yokocho.jpg "Photo: Dick Thomas Johnson, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons")

The narrow alley of tiny yakitori bars by Shinjuku station's west exit. Pick any counter with a free stool, point at what others are eating, order a beer. Cash is handy here.

## Shibuya crossing and the night

![Shibuya crossing at night](/media/routes/shibuya-crossing.jpg "Photo: Benh LIEU SONG, CC BY-SA 2.0, Wikimedia Commons")

Two stops on the JR Yamanote line. Cross the scramble once on foot, then watch it from above (the Starbucks above Tsutaya or Shibuya Sky). Finish in the small bars of Nonbei Yokocho, or just walk until the jet lag wins.
